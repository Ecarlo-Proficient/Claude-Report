## Cross-cutting observations

1. **The active footprint is ~5–10% of total volume.** Most of what's on these shares is historical archive. Map and reference accordingly — don't try to operate on full shares.
2. **Most Project #s are encoded in folder names.** CP-prefix on commercial, MFD-prefix on multifamily. This is the same Project # used in QBO and Notion — the join works.
3. **Project folders share a standard substructure** (PLANS, PM MISC, etc.) which means file-level tooling can rely on consistent paths.
4. **`Z - ` and `X - ` prefixes** in MULTY FAMILY DIVISION PROJECTS push folders to the bottom intentionally — useful filter convention to mirror in other places.
5. **PAYROLL folder mixes Proficient and MCP** — see scope note above.
6. **Field share is bid-archive only after exclusions** — bids are also referenced in Common/CURRENT PROJECTS/Commercial. Possible duplication or split-source risk; worth confirming where the "live" bid record lives.
