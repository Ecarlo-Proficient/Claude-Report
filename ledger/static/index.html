Draw race-through

The money flows one way: the GC funds the draw (money in to you) → you pay the vendors (money out) → each vendor sends an unconditional waiver → when all are in, turn the draw in to unlock the next. So "vendors paid" = you paid them; a draw is Ready to turn in once every bill is paid and every waiver is in hand. Click a tile above to filter. Check the box when a waiver arrives.
