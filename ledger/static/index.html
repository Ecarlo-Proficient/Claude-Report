Draw race-through

The money flows one way: the GC funds the draw (money in to you) → you pay the vendors (money out) → the GC pays your draw invoice. A draw is green (All paid) once the GC has paid you and every vendor is paid; Collect from GC means you've paid the vendors but the GC still owes you. Click a tile above to filter.
RP is excluded - residential bills at completion / milestones, not formal draws.

Open invoices

What the GC still owes you (your open AR), aged by due date into the same buckets as the AR Aging workbook - Current isn't due yet, 90+ is long overdue. The Lien column is the matching status from the Notion Lien Tracker (blank = no lien on file). Invoice # opens the invoice in QuickBooks. Click a bucket tile to filter.

AP & lien clock

These are unpaid bills YOU owe (your AP — money out). Texas gives your vendor/supplier a deadline (the 15th of the 2nd month after the bill) to send a lien notice; once it passes, they can put a lien on the project. So "past due" = pay these to clear the lien risk on your jobs. This is not your AR (you liening a GC who hasn't paid you).
